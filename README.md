## About Me

Software developer with 2+ years of experience building web applications across the frontend and backend, primarily with **TypeScript/JavaScript and Python**.

My experience includes **React, Next.js, Node.js, NestJS, Django, and Flask**, along with PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB, Docker, and Git. I enjoy working across the stack—from designing APIs and backend services to building maintainable frontend applications.

I hold a **Master's degree from VIT Vellore** and am currently focused on strengthening my engineering skills through personal projects and **open-source contributions**.
